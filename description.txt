Welcome to Xenon! Xenon is a mod that adds a variety of content, from vanilla expansions such as a larger ice biome
that extends into the lava layer to a new evil biome, the Corrosion. A semi-full list of content is below:

- The Rhyolite Cave biome (this is also its own mod)
- The Corrosion, an alternate to the Corruption and Crimson
- An expanded Ice biome, which extends into the lava layer and has a new tile called Frozen Lava
- Quicksand-type tiles for all sand variants, mud, and snow
- Various new enemies for some biomes
