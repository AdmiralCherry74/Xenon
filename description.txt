Welcome to Xenon! Xenon is a mod that is meant to be for those who prefer a slower paced gameplay, while still managing to make it engaging
There are a slew of changes, new content and planned features. For now this mod will only cover pre-Hardmode, however a Hardmode update is planned, and even a brand new 'Insane Mode'

Here's a few changes to really catch your eye!

Adds 4 new biomes:
- A newly erupted mini-biome generating in the roof of hell. The [b] Rhyolite Caves [/b] biome. This is also its own mod. Check out here to see! https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3467015068&searchtext=Rhyolite)
- An new acidic, stomach-themed alternative world evil called The Corrosion that seeks to eat your entire world and you for breakfast! Inspired but expanded on from the fan ideas wiki! https://terrariafanideas.fandom.com/wiki/The_Corrosion
- Scorching Ice, a new hot/cold mini-biome generating in the bottom of the Underground Snow / Ice biome! Watch out for Frozen Lava!
- Underground Ocean, an Ocean cave on the side of the jungle. its work in progress

Upcoming biomes:
- The Somnolent is an upcoming hallow alternative. Featuring a more somber, night, and calm theme as oppossed to typical world lights full of energy and shiny colours! make no mistake however as its just as deadly as its counterparts (Will Come out with the Hardmode Mode Update)
- The Catacombs is an upcoming dungeon alternative that is a Zombie themed slaughterhouse of a abandoned building with brand new accessories, weapons, enemies and more! (Will Come out with the Insane Mode Update)
- The Autumn is an upcoming jungle alternative that "EmeraldManJoe" made. It will feature Maple Trees, Ants taking place of Bee's, and bird people!? Is love in the air? if you thought so, wrong! maple leaf! (Will eventually come out)

Adds 6 new item types:
- Unique trinkets found through out the world that are called Valuables. They sell at great prices and even have their own rarity!
- Battleaxes are a new type of melee weapon that are slower, but have higher critical strike chance and inflict various new buffs to enemies and yourself!
- Crossbows, a new powerful but slow and costly style of ranged weapon.
- Splash potions, a way for mages to fight without mana! These potions are a new consumable throwing-styled weapon in which you use magical potions and buffs to take down enemies or heal allies!
- Caltrops are a new gadget for summons to play with to expand their arsenal against groups of enemies, inflicting normal and tag damage per one caltrop!
- Hoes are new tool used to collect from the soft and fragile tiles around you. being able to harvest unique materials, building blocks and more from grass, vines, thorns, and even Webs (plus way more)
