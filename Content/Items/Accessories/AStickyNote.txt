I (Emerald) feel like there needs to be some more Pre-HM accessories to help define classes. So I'm dumping some ideas here :3

    Spartian Charm - A melee focused accessory that increases Melee Damage based on how many enemies are near you.
    Might include with a Spartian Armor set made for a scrapped MC mod.

    Harpy Band - Hitting enemies releases small feathers that home in on other enemies.

    Mandible Necklace - Boosts Ranger Stats. Can be upgraded with Shark Tooth Necklace

    Backtank - Less ammo consumption and higher range for flamethrowers

    Poison Stinger - Whips deal more damage and inflict poison.

    Imp Shackles - An Upgraded Shackle with more defense and some Damage Reduction aswell as inflicting hellfire on Attackers

    Frost Relic - Gain a Snowflake ontop of your character that shoots iceballs at enemies.
    Note: May be placed in Scorching Ice

    Viking Horn Headband - Gain extra knockback and melee weapon size.

    Trailblaze Spur - Gain a small dash that works on mounts. Can be combined with Hellfire Treads in HM to make mounts faster

    Shooting Star - Dealing enough magic damage will cause a comet to fall on the enemy.
    Note: May be reteired for Somnolent
